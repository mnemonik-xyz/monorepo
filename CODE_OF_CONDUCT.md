# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributions from anyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level. The community works because participants extend each other good faith. This document sets the floor for behavior and the path for handling problems.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect. Disagree about ideas; do not attack people.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before reacting.
- Use inclusive language. Be considerate of non-native English speakers.
- Give credit where it is due. Cite prior work, prior reviewers, prior implementers.
- Keep technical discussions focused on the technical question.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments about any individual or group.
- Harassment in any form, including unwelcome contact after a request to stop.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, reviews, or community spaces.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, Discord, Telegram, mailing lists, conference appearances under the project's banner, and any other channel where someone is reasonably representing the project.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Send reports to **dev@mnemonik.xyz**. Include:

- What happened, where, and when (links if applicable).
- Who was involved.
- Whether the incident is ongoing.
- Whether you want your identity kept confidential from the reported party.

Reports are read by the project maintainers. We acknowledge receipt within 72 hours and aim to act within 7 days.

We will not retaliate against good-faith reporters, and we do not require a report to be polished or formal — a few sentences is enough.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may, at their discretion and proportionate to the incident:

1. **Private warning** — a clarifying message about the behavior and the expectation going forward.
2. **Public warning** — a posted note in the relevant channel.
3. **Temporary ban** — removal from a specific channel or from all project spaces for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal from all project spaces.

Maintainers will document the decision and the reasoning, and will keep the reporter informed of the outcome to the extent permitted by privacy considerations.

## Maintainer accountability

Maintainers are held to the same standard as contributors. A maintainer who violates this Code may be removed from their role by the remaining maintainers.
