# Task 7: Customer discovery and paid pilot

## Contract

[Product plan](../product-plan.md) and [user specification](../user-spec.md).

## What to do

Start interviews while implementation runs. Validate loss and migration pain, buyer and budget.
Record current alternatives, permanent-storage exclusions and reasons to decline.
Recruit design partners for one integration. Measure service and support costs.
Run paid recovery pilots after task 5 supplies release evidence.
Outreach requires a separate explicit instruction to contact people.

## Acceptance

Record interview evidence and decisions without exposing customer secrets.
Treat proposed counts as research targets, not factual demand.
Pass A-14 before claiming paid pilot validation.
Revise scope or pricing when evidence contradicts the hypothesis.

## Closure

Append evidence, measured costs and unresolved objections to the decision log.
A draft sales offer does not prove product demand.
