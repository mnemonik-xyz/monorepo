# Mnemonic Protocol — crawl policy
# The public surfaces (landing, ledger, analytics, blog) are meant to be
# indexed. The auth/signing surfaces are per-request flows with no public
# content, so compliant crawlers are kept off them (defense in depth — they are
# also absent from the sitemap). The sitemap lists the canonical public routes.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /sign/
Disallow: /oauth/

Sitemap: https://mnemonik.xyz/sitemap.xml
